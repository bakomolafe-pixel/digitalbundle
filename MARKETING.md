# Skill to Cash — Daily Marketing Playbook

> Goal: Turn this landing page into ₦100k+/day in sales — using free organic traffic from WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, Twitter (X), and Telegram.

---

## 1. The Core Message (Memorize This)

> **"People don't buy information. They buy a clearer version of themselves."**

Every post, story, reel, tweet, or DM you send must answer ONE buyer question:

> **"Can this finally help me make money?"**

If your content doesn't answer that — don't post it.

## 6. WhatsApp Status Strategy (Most Underrated Channel in Nigeria)

People don't admit it, but **WhatsApp Status converts higher than any IG post.** Why? It's intimate. It's already opened. It's people who know you.

**Daily Status Plan (post 4–6 statuses across the day):**

1. **Morning hook** — "Just woke up to another alert. ₦15k. I'll tell you how in my next status 👇"
2. **Mid-morning explainer** — Screenshot of payment + 1 sentence: "This came from the WhatsApp closing script in Bonus 5."
3. **Afternoon teaser** — Cover of the ebook + "If you're tired of posting and getting silence, this is for you."
4. **Evening proof** — Reshare a buyer's testimonial (with permission).
5. **Night CTA** — Direct link + "₦4,500 today only. Reply 'YES' for the link."

**Pro tip:** End each evening with: *"If anyone wants the link, type YES."* Then DM the link only to people who reply. This filters tire-kickers and warms up real buyers.

---

## 7. The DM/Closing Flow (Use Bonus 5 Scripts)

When someone messages you "Tell me more" or "How much?":

1. **Don't drop the link immediately.**
2. Ask: *"Quick one — what skill do you have right now that you'd love to monetize?"*
3. Listen. Reflect. Tie their answer to a chapter in the ebook.
4. Then send: *"This 25-page playbook + 5 bonuses (originally ₦47k) is ₦4,500 this week. I'll send the link if you're ready to actually start."*
5. Send link only after they confirm.

> **Why?** People who *commit verbally* before seeing a link convert 4–6x higher.

## 8. Paid Ads (When You're Ready to Scale)

Once you've made your first ₦50k organically, reinvest ₦15k–₦30k in:

- **Meta Ads (Instagram + Facebook)** — Boost your top-performing reel with the "Skilled but invisible" hook. Target: Nigeria, ages 22–45, interests: side hustle, freelance, online business.
- **TikTok Ads** — 15-second hook video → land them on the page.
- **Goal:** ₦500–₦1,500 cost per sale at ₦4,500 = 3x return minimum.
